My Journey

I've spent 5+ years working across the full design lifecycle — from leading design for complex web applications in regulated industries to conducting in-depth user research to usability testing for climate tech startups.

I've navigated startup culture, large 3,000+ employee organizations, academia, local franchise operations, and the highly regulated GMP industry. Along the way, I've learned how to balance rapid iteration with thoughtful, user-centered design and competing business goals.

I care deeply about people and work best in spaces where community matters and impact is at the heart of the project.

Personal challenges, including the loss of close family members, have deepened my empathy and resilience. I bring that groundedness to every project, every conversation, and every design decision.
